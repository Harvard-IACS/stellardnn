# Publication review — 14 November 2026

## Scope and interpretation

`data/recent-publications.json` contains 24 selected, verified recent works. It is an update inventory, not a complete career bibliography or a count of lab publications. Each record retains a primary source URL, author list, document type, verification date, and provenance note. Journal articles and workshop papers are distinguished from preprints. Existing historical publications should be preserved separately and should not be relabeled as independently verified by this review.

`year` is the journal/proceedings year where verified; otherwise it is the first preprint year. `date` has the meaning specified in `dateType`. In particular, Astromer 2 and multivariate embeddings were first submitted in 2025 but have 2026 journal references. Do not imply that a preprint submission date is a journal publication date. Publication and preprint versions are one work, not two publications.

- [Multivariate embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11637): current title is **Multivariate time series transformer embeddings for light curves of periodic variable stars**, not the shorter title shown in older snippets. arXiv supplies A&A 712, A168 (2026), DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202555941.

## Remaining review limits

Search engines expose materially different counts and incomplete coverage. Do not replace this with another unverified total. No citation count or h-index was updated.

The publisher access failures noted above limit final-version title verification for two astronomy papers. The inventory is suitable for a clearly labeled selection of recent papers; it is not suitable for asserting exhaustive coverage of 2025–2026 or the whole career.
