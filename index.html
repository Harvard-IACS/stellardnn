Research directions

Methods built around science.

All research ↗
01 / Scientific machine learning

Physics in the learning loop

Neural networks that solve differential equations, encode physical structure and quantify uncertainty. Work spans transfer learning, solution families and efficient training.

Explore this direction ↗ 02 / Astronomy & time series

Learning the language of starlight

Transformer representations of astronomical light curves, with work on transfer between surveys, limited-label classification and multiband time series.

Explore this direction ↗ 03 / Cybersecurity

Learning the patterns of malicious behavior

Language models and contrastive learning for behavioral malware detection, system-call analysis and security in IoT networks.

Explore this direction ↗ 04 / Scientific information

Making research more discoverable

Language models and retrieval methods for scientific literature, including work with the SAO/NASA Astrophysics Data System.
