Research directions

  • Astronomical light curves

    ASTROMER learns representations from changes in starlight over time.

  • Differential equations and physics

    The group uses neural networks to solve differential equations and model physical phenomena.

  • Machine learning for cybersecurity

    Research includes behavioral malware detection and security for routers and IoT networks.

The lab at Harvard SEAS

Led by Pavlos Protopapas, StellarDNN brings together machine learning and statistics to study astronomical and physical phenomena. The group uses neural networks and transformers to classify celestial objects, solve differential equations, and extract features from galaxy images and time series.
