NeuroDiffHub

A project for building and sharing neural-network solutions to differential equations.

Archived project

NeuroDiffHub was built on NeuroDiffEq to build, train, and share solvers for differential equations. Its archive featured Araz Sharma’s heat equation example, araz/heat_equation_rod1.

The project’s example loaded a pretrained one-dimensional solver, trained it further, and saved it to the hub.

# Load the pretrained solver
solver = Solver1D.load(name="project_name/equation_name")

# Train it further
solver.fit(max_epochs=1000)

# Save the retrained solver
solver.save(name="retrained_equation", save_to_hub=True)
