Astrophysics Data System machine learning

Language models for astronomy literature and observatory bibliographies.

astroBERT

The Astrophysics Data System (ADS) is a digital library for astronomy, astrophysics, and physics. This project uses ADS literature to train astroBERT and develop tools that identify named entities and surface related concepts in research papers.

  • Building astroBERT, a language model for Astronomy & Astrophysics
  • Improving astroBERT using Semantic Textual Similarity

Bibliography curation

Observatory bibliographies connect papers to the instruments and observations they use. We study whether language models can help curators distinguish a passing mention of a telescope from actual use of its data and identify the relevant datasets.

The WIESP Shared Task Challenge used papers from ADS and records from the Chandra Archive bibliography.
