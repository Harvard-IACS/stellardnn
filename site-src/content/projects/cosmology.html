Cosmology-informed neural networks

Neural network solvers for the equations of cosmic expansion.

Modeling cosmic expansion

We use neural networks to solve the equations for several cosmological models, including ΛCDM, a quintessence model with an exponential potential, and the Hu-Sawicki f(R) model.

A single network can learn a family of solutions across a range of model parameters. We use these solutions with observational data to estimate the parameters while reducing repeated numerical integration. The method is implemented in NeuroDiffEq.
